Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how PULSE may collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the PULSE website and services.

Information We Collect

PULSE may collect information you provide when creating or managing an account, completing tasks or surveys, submitting responses, or contacting us.

Account Information

Account information may include information necessary to identify and manage your PULSE account.

Task and Survey Information

Information submitted through tasks and surveys may be stored so submissions can be reviewed, documented, and associated with the appropriate account or task.

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to operate PULSE, manage accounts, process submissions, maintain records, provide notifications, improve services, prevent abuse, and support reporting and documentation.

Stored Records

PULSE may retain submission records, task information, account information, and related activity for as long as reasonably necessary for service operation, documentation, security, dispute resolution, or legal requirements.

Third-Party Services

PULSE may use third-party services to provide hosting, authentication, database, analytics, communications, or other website functionality. Information handled by those services may also be subject to their applicable privacy policies.

Security

PULSE uses reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. However, no online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children

PULSE is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the PULSE website, services, features, or legal requirements change.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled, please use the PULSE Contact page.
